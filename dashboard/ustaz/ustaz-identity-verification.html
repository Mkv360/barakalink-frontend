Optional verification

Verify your identity

Complete a quick live camera check to show that you are a real person and that your live face matches your existing BarakaLink profile photo.

It usually takes only a couple of minutes.

Why verify?

Stronger trust signal

Your profile can show an Identity Verified trust state once the server confirms the check passed.

May improve profile visibility

Verified identity is intended to become a stronger discovery signal when BarakaLink's future visibility system is enabled.

More confidence for families

The check helps confirm that the live person is the same person represented by the profile photo.

Verification is optional. It is not required to register, log in, complete your profile, use the Ustaz dashboard, or appear on BarakaLink.

What this verification means

  • It checks for a live person during the verification session.
  • It compares the live verification reference with your existing profile photo.
  • It does not independently verify your Islamic qualifications or teaching quality.
  • BarakaLink does not treat this check as a guarantee of teaching quality.
Maybe later

Your verification decision will be made by BarakaLink's secure verification service. You cannot make yourself verified from the browser.
