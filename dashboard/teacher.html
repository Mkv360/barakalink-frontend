Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

What is BarakaLink?

BarakaLink makes trusted Quran learning easier to find. Parents and students can discover Ustaz and Ustaza, save the teachers they like, send learning requests and communicate with them, all in one place.

How do I find an Ustaz or Ustaza?

Open Find Ustaz. Teachers are suggested using your study interests, your preferred teacher gender and your registered location, with nearby teachers shown first. You can filter by Ustaz, Ustaza or your study fields, search by name or study field, and save teachers to look at again later.

Is BarakaLink for online or in-person learning?

That depends on the individual Ustaz or Ustaza. BarakaLink shows nearby teachers first, but how and where lessons happen is something to check on their profile and confirm with them before you begin.

How do I request to learn from an Ustaz?

Find a teacher in Find Ustaz and send them a learning request. Requests you have sent are listed under My Learning, and you can cancel one if your plans change.

What happens after an Ustaz accepts my request?

Once a request is accepted, the Ustaz or Ustaza appears under Active Learning in My Learning, which lists your current teachers and learning connections. You can communicate with them from Messages.

How does messaging work?

Messages is where you talk with your Ustaz or Ustaza in one place. Choose a conversation to read it and write a reply. While a Parent account is still under review you can browse profiles, but messaging is unavailable until BarakaLink approves the profile.

What do the profile trust and verification indicators mean?

Trust indicators show verification steps that have been completed on a profile, such as submitting credentials or verifying identity. They are optional, so not every profile shows one, and they do not by themselves guarantee teaching quality or qualification. Please read them alongside the rest of the profile.

How do I report a problem or safety concern?

Open Report & Feedback, choose the topic that fits, including Safety or trust concern, and describe what happened. To report a specific Ustaz or Ustaza you can also use Report from the actions menu on their profile. If someone is in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency services first.

How do I contact BarakaLink Support?

Use the Ask BarakaLink form on this page for questions about using BarakaLink. For problems, safety concerns and ideas, use Report & Feedback instead.
