01 / Terms

Terms of Service

Welcome to BarakaLink. BarakaLink is a platform designed to help parents and students discover Quran teachers and to help Ustaz and Ustaza make their teaching services available to students and families.

By creating an account or using BarakaLink, you agree to follow these Terms of Service and the other policies linked on this page. If you do not agree with these Terms, please do not create an account or use the platform.

Important: BarakaLink is a connection and discovery platform. Unless BarakaLink expressly states otherwise for a particular service, BarakaLink does not itself guarantee a teacher's qualifications, teaching quality, conduct, availability, suitability, or learning results.

1.1 About BarakaLink

BarakaLink provides online tools that may include teacher discovery, profile pages, search and filtering, account management, verification, communication, and other features introduced over time.

Features can change as the platform develops. Some features may be unavailable, limited, or introduced only after additional terms are provided.

1.2 Accounts

To use account-based features, you must provide information that is accurate, current, and not misleading.

  • You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.
  • You must not impersonate another person or create a profile intended to deceive other users.
  • You must not knowingly provide false information about your identity, location, experience, qualifications, or teaching services.
  • You should contact BarakaLink promptly if you believe someone has accessed your account without permission.

1.3 Parent and Student Accounts

Parents and students may use BarakaLink to search for teachers, review teacher profiles, and connect with teachers for Quran learning.

Parents and guardians are responsible for making appropriate decisions about the teachers their children or dependants learn with.

When a learner is a child, the parent or legal guardian should remain appropriately involved in account use, communication, lesson arrangements, and in-person learning.

1.4 Ustaz and Ustaza Accounts

Ustaz and Ustaza may create teacher profiles and provide information about their teaching background, study fields, qualifications, languages, location, teaching format, availability, and related services.

A teacher is responsible for ensuring that the information presented through their profile is truthful, current, and not misleading.

  • Do not claim qualifications, certificates, study history, experience, or authorizations that you do not have.
  • Do not upload certificates or other documents that belong to someone else or that you are not authorized to use.
  • Do not upload a photo, audio recording, or other material that you do not have permission to use.
  • Keep your teacher profile reasonably up to date.

1.5 Teacher Verification

BarakaLink may use verification steps to confirm certain account or profile information.

A verification status means only that BarakaLink has completed the particular verification process applicable to that account or profile at that time.

Verification does not mean a guarantee. A verified status does not guarantee a person's qualifications, character, teaching quality, safety, conduct, availability, or suitability for a particular learner.

1.6 Teacher and Student Relationships

BarakaLink helps users find and connect with each other. The actual teaching relationship is between the student or parent and the teacher, unless BarakaLink expressly becomes a party to a separate service agreement.

Users should independently discuss lesson details, expectations, location, schedule, teaching approach, pricing, and other relevant arrangements before beginning lessons.

1.7 In-Person Learning

When users arrange in-person lessons, they are responsible for choosing an appropriate location and taking reasonable safety precautions.

Parents and guardians should use appropriate supervision for children and should consider whether a proposed learning environment is suitable.

1.8 Communication

Users must communicate respectfully and must not use BarakaLink or a connection made through BarakaLink to harass, threaten, exploit, deceive, pressure, or abuse another person.

Communication may be subject to reporting, moderation, investigation, or restriction where necessary to protect users, the platform, or the rights of others.

1.9 Profiles and User Content

Users may provide profile information, photographs, biographies, teaching descriptions, recitation recordings, qualifications, and other content supported by the platform.

You retain rights you have in your original content. By submitting content to BarakaLink, you give BarakaLink permission to store, process, reproduce, format, and display that content as reasonably necessary to operate and provide the platform.

You must not submit content that is unlawful, fraudulent, deceptive, abusive, infringing, or otherwise prohibited by these Terms or applicable law.

1.10 Prohibited Use

BarakaLink must not be used for conduct that threatens the safety, privacy, dignity, or rights of other users.

  • Fraud, scams, impersonation, or deliberate deception.
  • Harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or abusive conduct.
  • Sexual exploitation, grooming, or inappropriate conduct involving minors.
  • Sharing another person's private information without appropriate authorization.
  • False claims about teaching qualifications or professional credentials.
  • Attempting to gain unauthorized access to accounts, systems, or platform data.
  • Uploading malicious software or content intended to interfere with BarakaLink.
  • Using BarakaLink for purposes unrelated to the platform in a manner that harms users or the service.

1.11 Children and Minors

BarakaLink may be used in connection with Quran learning for children, but children require additional care when personal information and communication are involved.

Under Ethiopia's Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024, a minor is a data subject below the age of sixteen. Where minor personal data is processed, BarakaLink will apply safeguards and authorization requirements required by applicable law.

Parents, guardians, or tutors should provide any authorization required for a minor's use of the platform or processing of the minor's personal data.

Nothing in these Terms creates a payment obligation for a feature unless that feature and its applicable price are clearly presented to the user.

1.13 Reporting Problems

Users should report suspected fraud, harassment, unsafe conduct, account abuse, misleading profiles, or other serious violations to BarakaLink.

Reports should contain enough information for BarakaLink to understand and investigate the issue. Do not include unnecessary personal information when making a report.

02 / Privacy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information BarakaLink may collect, why we use it, how we protect it, and the choices and rights available to users.

BarakaLink aims to process personal information lawfully, fairly, transparently, and only for purposes that are reasonably connected to operating and improving the service.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with the BarakaLink Terms of Service, Safety information, and Community Guidelines.

2.1 Information We Collect

Depending on how you use BarakaLink, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and account information.
  • Phone number and email address.
  • Account role, such as Parent/Student or Ustaz/Ustaza.
  • Sub-city, area, and location-related information used for teacher discovery.
  • Nearest mosque or other location information that a user chooses to provide.
  • Teacher profile information such as gender, teaching experience, study fields, languages, biography, teaching approach, lesson format, availability, and other supported profile details.
  • Profile photographs.
  • Qualification certificates or other supporting documents uploaded by teachers.
  • Recitation recordings or other audio samples submitted by teachers.
  • Verification and authentication information, including information associated with Telegram verification where that method is used.
  • Messages, support requests, reports, or other information users voluntarily send to BarakaLink.
  • Technical information reasonably necessary to secure and operate the service, such as device, browser, IP, log, and security information.

2.2 How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and protect account security.
  • Verify accounts or specific teacher information where applicable.
  • Help parents and students discover teachers based on study field, location, gender, language, and other available information.
  • Display teacher profiles and related information required for the discovery service.
  • Provide customer support and respond to reports.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security incidents.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the platform.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

2.3 Public and Profile Information

Some information submitted by Ustaz and Ustaza may be displayed to other users because teacher discovery is a core BarakaLink feature.

Depending on the feature and profile settings, this may include a teacher's name, profile photo, teaching fields, general location, experience, languages, teaching approach, lesson format, availability, and other information intended to help students make an informed connection.

Users should not publish private home addresses, passwords, financial credentials, identity documents, or other information that does not need to be public.

2.4 Legal Bases and Consent

Personal information may be processed where permitted by applicable law, including where processing is necessary to provide a requested service, comply with legal obligations, protect legitimate interests where applicable, or where the user has provided valid consent.

03 / Safety

Safety

BarakaLink is designed to help people find Quran learning connections, but online and in-person interactions still require good judgment from every user.

3.1 For Parents and Guardians

  • Review a teacher's profile before arranging lessons.
  • Discuss teaching arrangements clearly before the first lesson.
  • For children, remain appropriately involved in communication and lesson arrangements.
  • Use suitable and reasonably safe locations for in-person lessons.
  • Do not share unnecessary financial, identity, password, or private household information.
  • Report suspicious, threatening, abusive, or inappropriate conduct.

3.2 For Ustaz and Ustaza

  • Keep your profile accurate.
  • Treat parents, students, and other teachers with respect.
  • Maintain appropriate professional boundaries.
  • Never request inappropriate photographs, personal information, money, or access to accounts.
  • Do not engage in sexual, exploitative, threatening, or manipulative conduct.
  • Take extra care when dealing with children and follow applicable safeguarding requirements.

3.3 In-Person Lessons

BarakaLink does not determine where users conduct lessons unless a specific feature says otherwise. Parents, guardians, students, and teachers should agree on suitable learning environments.

For children and younger learners, parents or guardians should use appropriate supervision and should not rely only on an online profile or verification badge when making safety decisions.

3.4 Reporting Safety Concerns

A user should contact BarakaLink when they believe another user is behaving dangerously, fraudulently, abusively, or in violation of the platform rules.

If a situation involves an immediate threat to life, physical safety, or a suspected crime, users should contact the appropriate emergency or law-enforcement authorities in addition to reporting the matter to BarakaLink.

3.5 What BarakaLink May Do

Depending on the circumstances, BarakaLink may review reports, request additional information, restrict content, suspend an account, remove a profile, disable communication features, or take other platform-level action.

We may not be able to disclose every detail of an investigation because doing so could compromise safety, privacy, security, or the rights of another person.

04 / Community

Community Guidelines

BarakaLink works best when parents, students, Ustaz, and Ustaza treat one another with respect, honesty, and appropriate boundaries.

4.1 Be Respectful

Communicate with patience and respect. Differences in teaching style, personal preferences, and learning needs should not become a reason for harassment or abuse.

4.2 Use Authentic Information

Profiles should represent real people and truthful information.

Teachers should not exaggerate experience or qualifications. Parents and students should also avoid misleading information when interacting with teachers.

4.3 Protect Personal Information

Do not publish or distribute another person's phone number, private address, identity documents, account credentials, photographs, recordings, or other private information without appropriate authorization.

4.4 No Harassment or Abuse

BarakaLink does not allow threats, stalking, intimidation, repeated unwanted contact, targeted harassment, or abusive behavior.

4.5 No Fraud or Scams

Do not use BarakaLink to deceive users, collect money through false claims, impersonate another person, create fake identities, or manipulate others through fraudulent offers.

4.6 Respectful Teacher-Student Boundaries

Teacher-student relationships should remain appropriate to the educational purpose of the platform.

Teachers and students should avoid conduct that could exploit trust, especially where a learner is a child or otherwise vulnerable.

4.7 No Exploitation of Children

Any sexual exploitation, grooming, manipulation, abusive conduct, or inappropriate contact involving a child is prohibited.

BarakaLink may take immediate platform action where such conduct is suspected and may cooperate with appropriate authorities where legally required or permitted.

4.8 Content Rules

Content submitted to BarakaLink should be relevant to the service and appropriate for a Quran learning community.

Do not submit unlawful, fraudulent, threatening, sexually exploitative, malicious, deliberately deceptive, or infringing content.

4.9 Religious Learning

BarakaLink provides technology for discovering and connecting with Quran teachers. BarakaLink does not independently establish every teacher's religious methodology, interpretation, qualification, or scholarly standing.

Users should review teacher information and choose learning arrangements that fit their own needs and beliefs.

4.10 Enforcement

Violations of the Community Guidelines may result in warnings, content removal, restrictions, suspension, or termination depending on the seriousness and circumstances of the conduct.

Serious safety concerns may result in immediate restrictions while the matter is reviewed.
